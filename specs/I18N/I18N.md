# 4 Localization and Internationalization

<!-- section:purpose -->
## Purpose and scope

Localization and Internationalization makes the platform usable in the languages, scripts, formats and time zones of every tenant and party, and keeps legally significant wording consistent and provable across languages. Tenants include arbitral institutions, SEBI ODR institutions and CORD tenants in India, Singapore and the US; their parties may read English, Hindi or other Indian languages, Chinese, or right-to-left scripts such as Urdu.

**In scope**: string externalisation, message format, translation workflow and RTL support ([[I18N-i18n]]); locale-aware dates, numbers, currency and time zones ([[I18N-l10n]]); per-tenant language packs and jurisdiction-specific legal document templates ([[I18N-content]]); the register of legal, declaration and consent texts ([[I18N-legal-declaration-and-consent]]); the platform glossary and terminology governance ([[I18N-platform-glossary-and]]).

**Out of scope**: deadline arithmetic and holiday calendars ([[PKG-calendars-and-deadline]]); tenant terminology override mechanics in settings ([[PKG-tenant-terminology-and-copy]]); multi-script fonts ([[PKG-typography-and-multi-script]]); voice and tone ([[APP-voice-tone-and-content-style]]); privacy notice content ([[SEC-privacy-notice-copy]]); document rendering engine ([[PKG-document-and-notification]]).

<!-- section:goals -->
## Goals and non-goals

Proposed targets:
1. 0 hard-coded user-facing strings in apps and packages (lint-enforced), including emails, PDFs and API problem text templates.
2. Every launch locale passes pseudo-localisation, RTL (where relevant) and truncation checks in CI before release.
3. Every displayed deadline shows date, time and time zone; 0 ambiguous numeric date formats.
4. Every legal text shown to a user is versioned, and the exact version and locale a user accepted can be reproduced for the life of the case.
5. A new locale can be added without code changes beyond message files and configuration.
6. Glossary terms are enforced in source strings and translation memory for all launch locales.

**Non-goals**: machine-translated legal or procedural text without human legal review; non-Gregorian calendars for legal dates; per-user custom terminology.

<!-- section:principles -->
## Principles

1. **Every user-facing string has a stable message ID** and lives in a message catalogue, never in code.
2. **ICU MessageFormat for plurals, selects and variables; no string concatenation.**
3. **Format with Intl and CLDR data, never by hand.**
4. **Store neutral, display local**: timestamps in UTC, money as integer minor units with ISO 4217 code, user text as Unicode as entered.
5. **Deadlines show the governing time zone** (seat time zone) and the viewer's local time where they differ.
6. **Legal text is versioned and owned**; what a user saw is recorded with version and locale.
7. **One concept, one term** from the glossary, in every language.
8. **Logical CSS properties and `dir` everywhere**, so RTL is a configuration, not a redesign.

### Ownership and governance

Proposed owner: frontend platform lead; content design owns glossary and copy rules; Nomology legal owns the legal text register; tenant legal approves tenant-owned texts.

<!-- check:boundaries -->
### Scope and boundaries

Calendars, fonts, tenant override mechanics and document rendering are owned elsewhere (listed in Purpose); this area owns externalisation, formatting, language packs, legal text register and glossary.

<!-- check:principles -->
### Principles and enforcement

Principles 1 to 3 are lint-enforced (no JSX literals, no concatenated messages, no hand formatting); 4 by Prisma schema conventions (timestamptz, minor units); 5 by the `Deadline` display component being the only way to render a deadline; 6 by the acceptance records schema; 7 by glossary lint; 8 by stylelint rules banning physical properties (`margin-left` etc.).

<!-- check:compliance -->
### Regulatory and control mapping

DPDP Act notice and consent language options for India; GDPR Article 12 clear and plain language; WCAG 2.2 AA Language of Page and Parts; provenance of accepted legal text for disputes about what a party agreed to.

<!-- check:regions -->
### Tenancy and residency

Locale data and catalogues are global code; tenant language packs, overrides and acceptance records are tenant-owned data stored in the tenant's home region.
