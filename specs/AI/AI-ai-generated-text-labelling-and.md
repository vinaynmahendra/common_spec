# 8.5.1 AI-generated text labelling and review

<!-- Generated layout: edit text between the markers; keep the markers and the front matter. -->

> Labels, human-approval rules and glossary / neutrality checks for AI summaries, drafts and captions

<!-- section:scope -->
## Scope and audiences

This node governs the words that tell people when text was produced or assisted by AI, and the content rules AI text must follow before a person approves it. It covers:

| Surface | Examples |
|---|---|
| In-app AI panels | Case summary panel (secretariat console), drafting assistance panel ([[APP-neutral-workspace]]), transcript suggestions ([[APP-transcriber-workspace]]) |
| Review screens | The approval step defined in [[AI-human-in-the-loop]] |
| Live and recorded captions | Automatic captions in the hearing room ([[DOM-hearing-room-accessibility]]) |
| Generated documents | Internal notes, and any procedural order or award where the institution requires an AI-assistance statement |
| Notifications | Only if a message drafted with AI help is sent; the label rule below applies |

**Readers:** secretariat staff and tribunal secretaries (expert, high volume), neutrals (expert, careful about their own responsibility), transcribers, and in a few cases parties and counsel (who see captions and, where an institution requires it, disclosure statements). Parties may include unrepresented SEBI ODR investors and MSME claimants with little legal knowledge.

<!-- section:voice -->
## Voice and tone

- **Plain and factual.** Say what the AI did and what the person must do: "AI draft. Check it against the documents before you use it."
- **Never overstate reliability.** No "accurate", "smart", "intelligent". Prefer "may contain errors".
- **Responsibility stays with the person.** Copy must make clear the reviewer decides: "You decide what goes into the order."
- **Neutral forum.** AI text must not appear to favour a party, predict an outcome or give legal advice. These are hard rules checked by guardrails ([[AI-guardrails]]) and reflected in prompts ([[AI-prompt-management]]).
- Follows the platform voice in [[APP-voice-tone-and-content-style]].

<!-- section:terminology -->
## Terminology and glossary alignment

| Canonical term | Meaning | Use | Banned synonyms |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI draft | Text generated by AI that nobody has approved yet | Labels on unreviewed output | "AI answer", "suggestion" (for full drafts), "auto-generated" |
| AI-assisted | Text a person approved after AI produced or changed it | Labels after approval | "AI-written", "machine-made" |
| Automatic captions | Machine speech-to-text shown live | Hearing room | "transcript" (a transcript is the reviewed record), "subtitles" |
| Reviewed by | The named person who approved | Approval line | "verified by", "certified by" (implies legal certification) |
| Source | A cited passage from a case document | Citations | "reference" when it means a passage |

Terms are entered in the platform glossary ([[I18N-platform-glossary-and]]); tenant variants are not allowed for these terms, because the label's meaning must be the same everywhere.

<!-- section:patterns -->
## Microcopy patterns

| Pattern | Rule | Good | Bad |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unreviewed label | Badge plus one sentence, shown above the text, never only colour | **AI draft.** Check it against the documents before you use it. | ✨ Smart summary |
| Approved label (internal) | Badge plus who and when | **AI-assisted.** Reviewed by Priya Rao, 12 Nov 2026 | Verified AI content |
| Citation chip | Document short name, version, page | Statement of Claim v2, p. 14 | [1] |
| Uncited sentence marker | Inline marker with text alternative | ⚠ No source found for this sentence | (red underline only) |
| Approve button | Verb plus effect | Insert into my draft | OK |
| Reject button | Clear verb | Discard draft | Cancel |
| Captions label | Persistent label on the caption area | Automatic captions. They may contain errors and are not the official transcript. | Live transcript |
| Empty result | Say what happened and what to do | No passages in this case's documents answer this. Try different words or check the bundle index. | No results |

<!-- section:errors -->
## Error messages

Structure: what happened, what to do. Never reveal model names, prompts or other cases.

| Code | Shown text |
|---|---|
| `AI_FEATURE_DISABLED` | AI assistance is turned off for your organisation. Your administrator can change this. |
| `AI_REGION_UNAVAILABLE` | AI assistance is not available right now in your region. You can carry on without it. |
| `AI_BUDGET_EXCEEDED` | Your organisation has reached its AI limit for this month. Your administrator can review the limit. |
| `AI_RATE_LIMITED` | Too many AI requests at once. Try again in a minute. |
| `AI_GUARDRAIL_BLOCKED` (grounding) | We could not support this draft with the case documents, so we have not shown it. Try a narrower question. |
| `AI_GUARDRAIL_BLOCKED` (content) | This request cannot be answered by AI assistance. |
| `AI_PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` | AI assistance is not responding. Try again later. Nothing in your case has changed. |

Codes follow the shared catalogue in [[APP-error-message-catalogue]] and problem types in [[APP-api-error-catalogue-problem]].

<!-- section:legal -->
## Legal and declaration text

| Text | Legal effect | Owner / approver | Versioning |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI-assistance statement in procedural orders or awards (if an institution requires one) | Disclosure to parties | Institution (tenant) approves wording; Nomology legal reviews the default | Tenant-configured, versioned; version recorded on the issued document |
| Captions disclaimer ("not the official transcript") | Limits reliance on captions | Nomology legal | Versioned in the legal text register ([[I18N-legal-declaration-and-consent]]) |
| Tenant opt-in notice shown to tenant admin when enabling AI | Records the tenant's decision | Nomology legal | Versioned; acceptance recorded ([[AI-ai-governance-and-impact]]) |
| Party-facing notice that AI is used in the service (privacy notice section) | Transparency under privacy law | Nomology legal with tenant | Part of [[SEC-privacy-notice-copy]] |

Assumption: no regulation in our regions currently prescribes exact wording for AI disclosure in arbitral or ODR documents; institutions may set their own rules. To be confirmed by legal.

<!-- section:notifications -->
## Notifications and generated documents

- Messages drafted with AI help are sent as the human sender's message after approval; no AI label in the message itself unless the tenant requires it (open question).
- Notifications never include AI-generated summaries of case content outside the platform; they link back to the case ([[PKG-notification-copy]]).

<!-- section:plain -->
## Plain language for non-lawyer parties

For any party-facing AI text (captions, disclosure statements): reading level around a 12-year-old's (proposed), short sentences, and one explanation of what "automatic" means: "Captions are made by a computer as people speak. They can be wrong. The official record is the transcript." See [[DOC-plain-language-guidance-for]].

<!-- section:localisation -->
## Localisation readiness

Labels and errors are externalised strings with stable IDs (`ai.label.draft`, `ai.label.assisted`, `ai.error.budgetExceeded`) in ICU format, for example `ai.label.assisted = "AI-assisted. Reviewed by {reviewerName}, {reviewedAt, date, medium}"`. When AI output is in a different language from the UI, the label is in the UI language. The English version is authoritative for legal texts unless the tenant designates another.

<!-- section:accessibility -->
## Accessible content

- Labels are text, not colour or icon only; badges have accessible names ("AI draft").
- The uncited-sentence marker has a text alternative read by screen readers.
- Label appears before the AI text in reading order, so screen-reader users hear it first.
- Caption area is labelled and can be resized; captions follow [[DOM-hearing-room-accessibility]].

<!-- section:governance -->
## Content governance and workflow

- Strings in this node are owned by the content design lead; legal-effect texts by Nomology legal.
- Changes ship with code (labels are locked strings; tenants cannot override them, see [[PKG-tenant-terminology-and-copy]]), except the tenant-configured AI-assistance statement, which tenant admins edit with preview and audit.
- Glossary and neutrality checks for AI text are part of the feature eval suites ([[TST-evals-and-ai-testing]]): banned-term checks on outputs and neutrality classifiers.

## Template checks

<!-- check:legal-accuracy -->
### Legal meaning and approval

Legal-effect texts are listed with owner and approver; the institution-specific AI-assistance statement is tenant-approved and versioned; the absence of prescribed regulatory wording is marked as an assumption for legal to confirm.

<!-- check:terminology -->
### Terminology consistency

Canonical terms (AI draft, AI-assisted, automatic captions, reviewed by, source) are in the glossary with banned synonyms, and tenant variants are not allowed for them.

<!-- check:tenancy -->
### Tenant-editable content safety

Labels and errors are locked strings; only the AI-assistance statement is tenant-editable, with preview, audit and versioning.

<!-- check:disclosure -->
### Confidentiality and privacy in copy

Notifications never contain AI summaries of case content; error messages never reveal model names, prompts or other cases.

<!-- check:provenance -->
### Version and proof of what was shown

The version of the AI-assistance statement used is recorded on each issued document; label version is recorded on each `ai_outputs` row; tenant opt-in acceptance is recorded with text version and timestamp.

<!-- check:ai-content -->
### AI-generated and assisted text

Inventory: case summaries, bundle summaries, drafting assistance, transcript suggestions, captions. Each has an unreviewed label ("AI draft") and an approved label ("AI-assisted. Reviewed by ..."); anything sent to parties or placed on the record requires approval ([[AI-human-in-the-loop]]); glossary and neutrality rules are in prompts and evals; captions carry a plain-language limit.
